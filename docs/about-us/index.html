How to join the collective

There are multiple ways to get involved.
You can become:

  • a Debater – submitting and debating theses voluntarily;
  • a Contracted Operator – work on projects of your choosing by submitting an Application for Coherent Participation to the project manager, with full autonomy under a contract offered by HARMONY連;
  • a Volunteer Operator – contribute voluntarily to non-profit, non-confidential projects, with coordination via known channels preferred.
