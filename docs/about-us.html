HARMONYREN is an Open Innovation Collective founded in Italy, dedicated to researching, proposing, debating, and turning ideas into reality.

HARMONYREN is currently in its pre-launch phase. If you’re interested, reach out to us at Contact Us.

Departments

To organize the domains where HARMONYREN operates, the following departments were created:

  • Department of Research and Development (RDD), our core domain
  • Department of Existential Operations (EOD)
  • Department of Game Development (GDD)
  • Department of Construction and Infrastructure (CID)
  • Department of Medicine (MED)
